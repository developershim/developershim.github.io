PresentationPro: Improving Public Speaking Skills through AI-Driven Virtual Reality Interactions · MOE2023-TRF-015

01 / The project

Why PresentationPro?

Public speaking improves through practice, but access to audiences, coaching, and realistic rehearsal environments can be limited. PresentationPro investigates a supplementary training approach using standalone VR and multimodal feedback.

Research question 01

University learning outcomes

To what extent can PresentationPro support the intended learning outcomes of university public-speaking training?

Research question 02

Workplace competencies

To what extent can PresentationPro help learners develop public-speaking competencies relevant to future professional settings?

Potential educational and training benefits

Practice on demand

Rehearse independently without needing a physical audience for every session.

Behavioral awareness

Reflect on gaze, gestures, vocal volume, and use of presentation space.

Instructor support

Use practice evidence to complement coaching and classroom instruction.

03 / Get the app

Install on Meta Quest 3 / 3S

The research prototype was developed for Meta Quest 3. Quest 3S installation may be possible, but compatibility with that model has not yet been verified. APK distribution is available by request rather than through a public download link.

Request APK access
Request an authorized APK build

Contact the project investigator for the current build, compatibility notes, and any conditions of access. Do not install APKs from unofficial sources.

Prepare your headset and computer

Update your Quest headset. For sideloading, enable Developer Mode for your headset through Meta's supported developer setup process, and connect the headset to a computer with a USB data cable.

Install using a trusted sideloading tool

With Android Debug Bridge (ADB) installed and the headset authorized for USB debugging, run adb devices to confirm the connection, then adb install -r PresentationPro.apk using the actual filename and path provided.

Launch and calibrate

Find the installed application in your headset's apps library (the location of sideloaded apps varies by Quest software version). Follow the application's calibration and safety instructions before starting a session.

Safety and access: Use a clear play area and stop if you experience discomfort. Developer Mode and sideloading requirements can change. Distribution and device compatibility should be confirmed with the research team before installation.
What if ADB does not detect my headset?

Check that your USB cable supports data, the headset is unlocked, and you accepted the USB debugging authorization prompt inside the headset. Reconnect and run adb devices again.

04 / Research findings

What the formative study found

An exploratory, single-arm study followed ten university learners during four weeks of at-home VR practice. Nine working-professional alumni subsequently assessed live presentations. Results below are aggregated and do not identify individual participants.

What worked

The study demonstrated that learners could independently operate the system and complete a structured schedule of VR rehearsal. Onboarding was generally rated favorably.

What still needs work

Some learners experienced VR discomfort, and practice was clustered on relatively few days. Professional feedback highlighted needs in gaze and movement, plus audience engagement, slide design, and language beyond the current system's feedback coverage.

Important interpretation: This was a formative study without a control group. The results support feasibility and inform future design, but do not establish that PresentationPro caused improvements in speaking performance or that skills transferred to workplace settings. The percentages above describe improvement comments, not percentages of participants.
